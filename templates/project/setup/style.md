## Graphics Guidelines

### Intensity and Frequency
- **Intensity:** selective
- **Target Budget:** 1 per act, maximum 6-8 across book

### Rendering Styles

#### Artistic Rendering Style
- **Medium:** Pen-and-ink with fine cross-hatching and linework
- **Palette:** Monochromatic black ink on aged cream / off-white paper
- **Tone:** Atmospheric, high shadow contrast, somber realism

#### Technical Rendering Style
- **Medium:** Admiralty hydrographic survey cartography and period engineering patent drafting
- **Palette:** Crisp black linework with subtle stippling and contour hatching
- **Typography:** Serif cartographic labels, compass rose, scale bar

### Usage Rules and Triggers

#### When to Use Graphics (Triggers)
- Introduce a chart or map whenever the narrative enters a new geographic territory or tactical space.
- Introduce an architectural cutaway or schematic when physical layout or machinery operation is critical to beat stakes.
- Introduce an in-world document facsimile when an encrypted message or official document is examined as a primary plot clue.

#### When NOT to Use Graphics (Negative Rules)
- Avoid illustrating conversational dialogue or character emotional reactions.
- Never depict an object or location before the POV character discovers it.

### Additional Specifications

#### Placement and Layout
- **Placement:** Chapter opening header for geographic maps; inline between scene beats for schematics and in-world artifacts.

#### Aspect Ratio and Dimensions
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 for cartography and landscape plates; 1:1 for chapter header vignettes.

#### Text Labeling and Localization Policy
- **Policy:** Text-minimal where feasible. For cartographic maps with place names, provide localized assets in `publishable/graphics/` (`ILL-NNN.<locale>.png`).

#### Visual Continuity and Symbol Library
- **Continuity:** Maintain identical symbol keys and line-weight hierarchy across all map assets.

## Publication & Book Design Guidelines

### Format and Delivery Targets
- **Formats:** epub, pdf
- **Cover Asset:** publishable/graphics/cover.png

### Page Layout & Trim Size (PDF & Print)
- **Trim Size:** 5.5in x 8.5in
- **Inner Margin:** 0.75in
- **Outer Margin:** 0.5in
- **Top Margin:** 0.75in
- **Bottom Margin:** 0.75in
- **Chapter Openings:** recto
- **Running Headers:** recto-verso

### Interior Typography & Typesetting
- **Body Font:** EB Garamond
- **Heading Font:** Cinzel
- **Font Size:** 11pt
- **Line Height:** 1.35
- **Paragraph Indent:** 1.25em
- **Scene Break:** * * *
- **Drop Caps:** true
- **Justification:** true
- **Hyphenation:** true

### EPUB & E-Reader Specifications
- **Reflowable:** true
- **TOC Depth:** 2
- **Dark Mode Support:** true
- **Max Image Width:** 1200px
